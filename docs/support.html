Common questions

My readiness score says --

That means nothing has been logged yet today, or the check-in had no usable values. The app shows a dash rather than inventing a number from no data. Check in and it will fill.

Can I get my data onto a new phone?

Yes. Settings → Export a backup writes a JSON file and hands it to your share sheet. On the new device, Settings → Restore from a backup. Restoring replaces everything on that device rather than merging, because merging two histories would duplicate sessions and corrupt every score after them.

I logged a session by mistake

Sessions tab, swipe the row left, tap Delete. A mis-logged session inflates your training load and skews readiness until you remove it, so it is worth fixing.

Why does iOS not import my sleep?

On iOS the app currently reads resting heart rate from Apple Health but not sleep, so sleep is entered by hand. On Android, Health Connect provides both. We are closing that gap.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops renewal; you keep access until the current period ends, and all your data stays on your device either way.

I deleted the app and lost my history

Everything is stored on the device, so uninstalling removes it and there is no copy for us to restore — we never had one. Export a backup before reinstalling or changing phones.
