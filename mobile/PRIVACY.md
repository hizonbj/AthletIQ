# AthletIQ privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-29_

AthletIQ does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, and no
analytics. This document exists because app stores require one, and because the
short version is worth stating plainly.

## What AthletIQ stores

Everything you enter — your check-ins (sleep, sleep quality, soreness, energy,
resting heart rate), the sessions you log, and the record of when you trained
above what your readiness endorsed — is written to a database **on your device**
and nowhere else.

We cannot read it. There is no copy on a server, because there is no server.

## Health data

If you turn on health import, AthletIQ reads two things from Apple Health or
Android Health Connect:

- Sleep duration
- Resting heart rate

These are read only, used only to fill in check-in values you have not entered
yourself, and stored on your device alongside the rest of your data. AthletIQ
never writes to Health, and never transmits health data anywhere.

You can decline this permission and use the app normally by entering the values
yourself.

## Notifications

If you turn on the morning reminder, it is scheduled locally by your device.
Nothing is sent through a push server, so no notification you receive from
AthletIQ has passed through us.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat, which manages
subscription state. They receive the information required to process and
validate a purchase — an anonymous subscriber identifier and the receipt from
the store. They do not receive your check-ins, your sessions, or your override
history. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for how they handle that data.

We do not receive your payment details from anyone.

## Backups

You can export your data as a JSON file from Settings. That file is handed to
your device's share sheet, and where it goes from there is entirely your choice.
Once it leaves the app, its privacy is governed by wherever you put it.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No third-party SDK receives your training data
- Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling AthletIQ deletes everything, because everything is local. There is
nothing to request from us and nothing for us to erase. Export a backup first if
you want to keep your history — once the app is gone, so is the data.

## Not a medical service

AthletIQ records training decisions and reports patterns in your own numbers. It
is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, or predict injury or
illness. Nothing in it is medical advice.

## Children

AthletIQ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
anything from them — or, for that matter, from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes — in particular if a future version adds an account
or sync, which would genuinely change the answers above — the new policy will be
published with the release that introduces it, and the change will be called out
in the app rather than quietly amended here.
