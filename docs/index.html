Your score is free. Always.

Every wearable already gives you a recovery number. What none of them keep is a record of the mornings that number said back off and you trained anyway — and what that decision actually cost you.

How it works

  1. Check in each morning. Four taps, no keyboard.
  2. You get a readiness score and the intensity it endorses.
  3. Log a session. If it is harder than endorsed, that is an override, and it goes on the record.
  4. A few days later it settles: your readiness afterwards, measured against your own baseline. That difference is what it cost.
  5. Next time you reach for a hard session on a low day, you are told what the last four cost you — before the session, not after.

Your data stays on your device

No account, no server, no analytics. Nothing to leak, because nothing is collected. See the privacy policy.
